## 1. Question
What information exists at the confirmed close BEFORE an objective large XAUUSD move, and can a deterministic SETUP → TRIGGER engine detect it at the time it is available, with positive after-cost economics? Not a threshold re-tune of MC/PB/BO/SR/MR.

## 2. Information-time rule (hard)
Every setup/trigger feature at decision candle t uses bars ≤ t and replay-row context recorded at the close of t only. Swings are pivots with 3 left / 3 right bars and become known at pivot + 3. Forward movement is used ONLY to label move events after the fact and never enters a feature. Controls: leak (future-direction entries ≫ 0), null (random direction ≈ 0), first exit bar ≥ t + 1, a "prefix invariance" test (features of bars ≤ t unchanged when later bars are altered).

## 3. Large move event (reused from V1–V3, judged valid; limitation noted)
Onset k = last confirmed close before a move: from close[k], within 24 bars the favourable excursion reaches ≥ 3 × ATR14(k) before the adverse excursion reaches 1 × ATR14(k); direction = the side that qualifies first; non-overlapping (next scan starts at k + 24); session gaps (> 3 h) break the window. Recorded per event: start k, end (bar where 3 ATR was reached), direction, magnitude (max favourable excursion within 24 bars, USD and ATR), time-to-move (bars from k to 3 ATR). Limitation: ATR14 is a simple 14-bar mean; onset is the last pre-move close, so a trigger at k is "at onset", k + 1 … k + 6 "late but inside the capture window". The definition is frozen here and not changed after results.

## 4. Pre-move feature set (computed at every bar; researched at k and averaged over k − 6 … k − 1; baseline = all in-range candles)
atr14; atr percentile over the last 288 bars; 12-bar and 24-bar range in ATR; range contraction = 12-bar range / previous 12-bar range; mean 6-bar overlap; body ratio; close location; bar range in ATR; EMA20 slope over 6 bars in ATR; 6-bar and 12-bar momentum in ATR (close − close[−n]); momentum acceleration (mom6 − mom6[−3]); EMA20 vs EMA50 state; run length of closes on one side of EMA20; distance to last confirmed swing high / low in ATR; swing width in ATR; equal highs/lows within 0.2 ATR among confirmed swings of the last 48 bars; bars since the last sweep (bar beyond a confirmed swing that closed back inside); bars since the last failed breakout (close beyond a swing then back inside within 3 bars); 5m regime, 15m bias, 30m and 1H regime from the replay row. Reported as base rate vs pre-move rate (lift) and per-quintile economics of pooled V4 trigger signals. No composite score.

## 5. Setup families and trigger variants (deterministic; parameter FAMILIES are small frozen grids, one value chosen on DEV by §8; BUY and SELL mirrored and reported separately; no side dropped)
Common: entry = close of the trigger bar; structural stop per family, floored at 0.5 ATR and rejected (TRIGGER_BLOCKED:RISK) above 3.0 ATR; TP = entry ± 1.70 × risk; ATR14 ≥ 2.0 USD; chase guard per family. Setup state must hold at t − 1 (the bar before the trigger) unless stated.
- **A COMPRESSION → EXPANSION.** Setup: 12-bar range / ATR14 ≤ wA, wA ∈ {2.0, 2.5, 3.0}, and ATR percentile(288) ≤ 0.5. Triggers: A1 = bar range ≥ xA × ATR (xA ∈ {1.5, 2.0}) with close beyond the 12-bar range and close location ≥ 0.7 (BUY) / ≤ 0.3 (SELL); A2 = A1 and close beyond the last confirmed swing in the direction; A3 = range ≥ xA × ATR with directional close location only. Stop = opposite end of the compression range.
- **B LIQUIDITY SWEEP → RECLAIM.** Setup: a bar j traded beyond the last confirmed swing high (SELL) / low (BUY) known at j − 1 and closed back inside; age t − j ≤ aB, aB ∈ {3, 6}. Triggers: B1 = the sweep bar itself (close back inside); B2 = first later close beyond the sweep bar's opposite extreme (confirmation). Stop = sweep extreme + 0.1 ATR. Chase: close must be within 1.5 ATR of the swept level.
- **C STRUCTURE BREAK → CONFIRMATION.** Break = first close beyond the last confirmed swing high (BUY) / low (SELL). Triggers: C1 = the break bar; C2 = second consecutive close beyond (break-and-hold); C3 = retest within rC bars (rC ∈ {6, 12}): a bar whose low ≤ level + 0.3 ATR (BUY) that closes above the level. Stop = last confirmed swing low (BUY) / high (SELL). Chase: break-bar close within 1.5 ATR of the level.
- **D PULLBACK → CONTINUATION.** Context: EMA20 > EMA50 and EMA50 rising over 12 bars (BUY). Setup: pullback ≥ dD ATR (dD ∈ {0.8, 1.2}) from the 20-bar high reaching within 0.3 ATR of EMA20, with the last confirmed swing low intact. Triggers: D1 = close > previous high and > EMA20 within 3 bars of the pullback low; D2 = two consecutive closes above EMA20 after the pullback. Stop = pullback low.
- **E MOMENTUM IGNITION.** Setup (quiet): |mom6| at t − 3 ≤ 0.5 ATR and 12-bar range ≤ 3 ATR. Trigger: mom3 ≥ mE ATR (mE ∈ {1.0, 1.5}), acceleration > 0, body ratio ≥ 0.6, close location ≥ 0.75 (BUY). EARLY/LATE: displacement from the 12-bar low (BUY) ≤ 2.0 ATR, else rejected as LATE_EXHAUSTION. Stop = lowest low of the last 3 bars.
- **F RANGE BREAK → ACCEPTANCE.** Setup: 36-bar range width ≤ wF ATR (wF ∈ {3, 4}). Triggers: F1 = first close outside the range with close location ≥ 0.6; F2 = second consecutive close outside (acceptance); re-entry inside the range before F2 = REJECTION (no trade). Stop = boundary − 0.5 ATR (re-entry = failure).
- **G TREND RESUMPTION.** Context: EMA50 rising over 24 bars, close > EMA50, 30m regime (row) = BULL_TREND (BUY, mirrored). Setup: pullback to EMA20 ± 0.3 ATR without a close below EMA50; last confirmed swing low intact. Trigger: mom3 ≥ 0.5 ATR and close > previous high; displacement from the pullback low ≤ dG ATR (dG ∈ {1.0, 1.5}). Stop = pullback low.
