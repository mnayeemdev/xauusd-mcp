## 5. Frozen comparable exit (EXIT_F) for EVERY entry candidate and every ablation
Entry at the signal close (BUY + spread; SELL at bid). Structural stop = engine `sl`. Broker fail-safe SL = 1.5 × |entry − sl| + spread from the fill (production `computeProtectiveStops`), intrabar. Thesis invalidation = first CONFIRMED close beyond `sl` → exit at that close. Target = engine `tp2` (touch, intrabar; if SL and TP touch in the same bar, SL first). Horizon 288 bars → exit at close. No adaptive management, no harvesting, no trailing (exit optimisation must not rescue entries). R unit = planned structural distance d = |entry − sl|; 0.01-lot USD = R × d. Costs: NORMAL spread 0.24 / slippage 0.10 per exit; STRESS 0.60 / 0.20; DRIFT = fill at the next bar's open; swap −0.56 USD per night on BUY. Cost burden reported in USD/trade, R, and % of gross expectancy.
