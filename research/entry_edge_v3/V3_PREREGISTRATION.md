## 5. Regime hysteresis family (frozen; V2 regime taxonomy unchanged; no new regimes)
Raw regime r(t) = production 5m regime at the confirmed close. Hysteresis regime H_k(t): H_k(t) = H_k(t−1) unless r(t−j) = r(t) for all j = 0..k−1 and r(t) ≠ H_k(t−1), in which case H_k(t) = r(t). H0 = raw (k = 0, V2 behaviour); H1 = one confirming bar (k = 1 → identical to raw by construction, retained as the formal lower neighbour); H2 = two consecutive bars; H3 = three consecutive bars. The sequence resets at the first candle of each session. Threshold-band hysteresis (H3 of the directive's example) cannot be computed from the replay rows (raw ADX/BB values are not stored) and is therefore replaced by the three-bar confirmation as the upper neighbour; this limitation is reported. Hysteresis effect metrics vs H0: regime flips per session, wrong-direction signals (CONTROL signals opposite to a move event within ± 6 bars) under regime-conditioned eligibility, missed valid signals (winning signals removed by the hysteresis-conditioned rule that H0 admitted), trade count, expectancy, PF, DD, cost, move capture. Hysteresis level selection for a finalist: the level with the highest DEV expectancy among H0–H3 with DEV N ≥ 100, accepted only if it is ≥ H0 on expectancy AND ≤ H0 on DD; otherwise H0.
